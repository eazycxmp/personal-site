Running count — add these up as cards come out. True count — running count ÷ decks still in the shoe. That's the number that says how rich the shoe really is.

Why deck count changes everything
  • 1 deck — every card is 2% of the shoe. The count swings violently and one true count point is worth a lot. Hardest to beat because the shuffle comes fast.
  • 2 decks — still sharp swings, and true count ≈ running count for most of the shoe.
  • 4 decks — the middle ground. Divide by decks remaining and the count starts to matter more than the raw running number.
  • 6 decks — the casino standard. Slow, small edges; a +6 running count with 5 decks left is only a true +1.2 and means almost nothing.

The rules from your seat

How the game works
  • Beat the dealer without going over 21. You only ever play against the dealer.
  • Face cards = 10. Ace = 11 or 1, whichever helps you.
  • Hit take a card. Stand keep your total.
  • Double double your bet and take exactly one more card.
  • Split a pair becomes two hands, each with its own bet.
  • Blackjack (ace + ten on your first two) pays 3 to 2.
  • The dealer has no choices — they hit to 16 and stand on all 17s. That's why the dealer's up card tells you so much.
Basic strategy in one breath
  • Hard 17+: stand. Hard 12–16: stand vs dealer 2–6, hit vs 7–A.
  • Hard 11: double. Hard 10: double vs 2–9. Hard 9: double vs 3–6.
  • Soft 18 (A,7): double vs 3–6, stand vs 2/7/8, hit vs 9/10/A.
  • Always split aces and 8s. Never split 5s or 10s.
